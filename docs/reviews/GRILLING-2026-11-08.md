## Context

FEAT-001 (contributions) and FEAT-003 (organizer identity) are implemented, but FEAT-002 (payouts) was blocked by **5 critical architectural gaps** exposed during grilling:

1. **Race condition safety** (concurrent webhook confirmation)
2. **Currency model** (implicit GHS vs. explicit currency in Money type)
3. **Late payment handling** (payment after goal closure)
4. **Refund timing** (pre-payout vs. post-payout policy)
5. **Settlement tracking** (confirmed ≠ settled; payout eligibility)

Each gap had **hidden assumptions** that would have caused production bugs, data inconsistencies, or impossible-to-enforce constitution violations if deferred to "we'll figure it out during implementation."

---

## Decisions Made (Binding)

### Decision #14: Concurrency Safety

**Question:** Does `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` in READ COMMITTED prevent double-posting?

**Answer:** YES. Verified that PostgreSQL READ COMMITTED with `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on the contribution row is sufficient. Second transaction waits, reads updated state, transition returns empty, ledger post is skipped. Idempotency key constraint is second layer (defense in depth).

**Action:** Add concurrent integration test (T210-T214) to prove it.

**Artifacts:** Test task in `FEAT-002-BLOCKERS.md`.

---

### Decision #15: Currency-Aware Money (ADR 0011)

**Question:** Keep GHS implicit or commit to currency field now?

**Answer:** **Commit now.** Replace `long pesewas` with `long minorUnits` + `String currencyCode`. GHS remains sole supported currency for MVP, but schema and type are ready for Nigeria/USD.

**Why it matters:** Deferring this forces a breaking migration when multi-currency launches (change every financial table, every Money construction). Doing it now is one-time pain; deferring is compounding debt.

**Cost:** Find/replace `Money.ofPesewas(x)` → `Money.of(x, "GHS")` in ~50 call sites.

**Artifacts:**
- `docs/adr/0011-currency-aware-money.md` (Accepted)
- `backend/src/main/resources/db/migration/V5__currency_and_settlement.sql`
- Tasks T201-T209 in `FEAT-002-BLOCKERS.md`

### Decision #16: Late Payment After Closure (ADR 0013)

**Question:** What happens when Paystack confirms payment after organizer closes goal?

**Answer:** **Suspense account + reconciliation flag.** Payment still transitions to CONFIRMED (Constitution I.5: never drop confirmed money), but posts to `SUSPENSE` ledger account instead of goal pool. Flag `requires_reconciliation = true`. Operator investigates and either refunds, reclassifies to goal pool (with approval), or records as misc income.

**Why it matters:** Automatic goal pool increase would let organizers re-open and withdraw late payments, bypassing trust safeguards. Suspense prevents silent balance inflation.

**Artifacts:**
- `docs/adr/0013-refund-and-loss-allocation.md` (Accepted)
- New `SUSPENSE` and `PLATFORM_LOSS` ledger accounts in V5 migration
- Updated `specs/domain/contribution-state-machine.md` with reconciliation transition
- Tasks T222-T228 in `FEAT-002-BLOCKERS.md`

### Decision #17: Refund Policy (ADR 0013)

**Question:** Can organizer refund after payout? Who eats the loss if beneficiary won't return money?

**Answer:** **Timing-based policy with explicit loss allocation.**

- **Before payout:** Normal REVERSAL posting, contribution → REFUNDED, goal pool decreases.
- **After payout:** Enters **manual recovery workflow**. Platform attempts beneficiary clawback (if contract permits). If recovery fails, operator creates `LOSS_ALLOCATION` entry crediting `PLATFORM_LOSS` account with approval reason.

**Why it matters:** Assuming clawback always succeeds is naive. Assuming platform always eats loss is unsustainable. Explicit allocation tracks fault (provider error vs. fraud vs. platform mistake) and enforces accountability.

**Artifacts:**
- `docs/adr/0013-refund-and-loss-allocation.md` (Accepted)
- Tasks T250-T256 in `FEAT-002-BLOCKERS.md`
- `INV-LEDGER-003` test remains disabled until FEAT-002 implements payout-aware refund check

### Decision #20: Settlement Tracking (ADR 0012)

**Question:** How do we enforce "payout only from settled funds"?

**Answer:** **Separate settlement tables.** New `settlements` and `settlement_items` tables track Paystack settlement batches. Contributions link to settlement items. Payout eligibility checks **settled balance** (sum of `SETTLED` items minus prior payouts), not confirmed balance.

**Why it matters:** Paystack settles T+1. Organizer requests payout same day; we have no cash. Without settlement tracking, payout request succeeds but Paystack transfer fails (insufficient balance). Leads to stuck payouts, manual fixes, broken trust.

**Architecture:** Settlement is **cash flow**, ledger is **obligations**. Keep them separate. Future ADR may add settlement postings to ledger for full audit, but not MVP blocker.

**Artifacts:**
- `docs/adr/0012-settlement-tracking.md` (Accepted)
- `specs/domain/settlement.md` (new domain spec)
- `settlements` and `settlement_items` tables in V5 migration
- Tasks T215-T221, T240-T244 in `FEAT-002-BLOCKERS.md`
